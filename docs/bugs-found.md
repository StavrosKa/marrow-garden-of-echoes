# Bugs found

Real bugs only (tech.md). Each entry gives the seed, what broke, the regression test and the fix commit, cited by its subject line.

## 2026-11-16 - Comfort grid: samples at a cell edge counted one cell off

- Seed: none. Not found by fast-check: a Node script compared `min(floor(v * n), n - 1)` with the edges `k / n` at every grid size from 1 to 256, the range validateTuning allows for `gridCols` and `gridRows`.
- What broke: `cellIndex` in `packages/marrow/src/core/comfortGrid.ts` (task 6.1; the bug never reached a commit) found a sample's cell with `min(floor(v * n), n - 1)`, while `cellRectCamera` puts the edge between cells k - 1 and k at `k / n`. The two are rounded separately and sometimes disagree by one cell, so a cell could become valid from samples outside its own rectangle, against design §9.2.
  - At the shipped 24 rows, the largest double below the row edges 5, 10, 17, 20 and 23 was counted in the row after the edge, not the row before it. For example, y = 0.20833333333333331, the double just below 5 / 24, was counted in row 5 although it lies in row 4's rectangle. The shipped 32 columns agreed at every edge.
  - At 167 of the 256 sizes, a point exactly on an edge was counted in the cell before it: at 22 columns, x = 15 / 22 was counted in column 14, not 15; at 49, (1 / 49) * 49 = 0.9999999999999999. At 212 sizes the largest double below an edge was counted in the cell after it, and 218 sizes had at least one of the two.
- Fix: `cellIndex` takes `i = floor(v * n)`, moves it down by one if `v < i / n` and up by one if `v >= (i + 1) / n`, then applies `min(i, n - 1)`. Req 11.2 and design §9.2 each gained one sentence: the cell of a point is the cell whose half-open rectangle, as computed, contains it, with x = 1 and y = 1 in the last cell.
- Regression tests, in `packages/marrow/src/core/comfortGrid.test.ts`, each run for every `gridCols` and every `gridRows` that validateTuning allows: a point exactly on each internal edge is counted in the next cell; the largest double below each internal edge is counted in the cell before it; and each of those points, plus 0, the largest double below 1 and 1, is counted in the cell whose `cellRectCamera` rectangle holds it. All six failed before the fix, at 167, 212 and 218 sizes for each axis, and pass after it.
- Fix commit: "Add comfort grid".

## 2026-11-17 - Placement: a disc with a mapped extreme exactly on a non-valid cell's edge was accepted

- Seed: 29, input 340, of 1000 inputs from `fc.sample` of the inputs of `placement_covers_every_point` (task 7.2). It was not a failing property run: running that input generator over seeds 1 to 120 (120,000 inputs, 1,119,256 slots) against the committed `placement.ts` gave one input whose strict sampled layer failed, and this is it. In vitest, `placement_covers_every_point` and its siblings passed on every run before that.
- What broke: `cameraEllipse` in `packages/marrow/src/core/placement.ts` (task 7.1, commit "Add slot placement", already pushed) took each semi-axis from the +R side only, `|toCamera(slot + (R, 0)).x - cx|`, and compared `cx - rx` and `cx + rx` with the frame. `toCamera` rounds the two sides differently, so for a disc whose mapped extreme lies exactly on a cell's edge the two distances differ by one ULP, and the gap to the cell could come to 1.0000000000000047 radii, just outside the cell test. The slot was accepted although a point of its disc lies on the edge, which belongs to a cell that is not valid, against Req 14.2.
  - Seed 29, input 340: the mapping has A = 2.4, a = 1.0161218350811259, g = 1.6 and centre (1e-323, 1); the demand has r = 0.13, m = 0.05 and n = 1; the left wrist's grid is valid in columns 1 to 4 of rows 20 to 23 and not valid at cell (5, 22). Slot 3, (3.4200000000000004, 1.8533450438705228), is the centre of cell (3, 22) mapped to screen. Its camera centre is 0.10937499999999989, its extremes on x are 0.0625 and 0.15625, which is exactly the edge x = 5 / 32 of cell (5, 22), and the distances to them are 0.04687499999999989 on the +R side and 0.04687500000000011 on the other. The committed code accepted the slot and `placeTargets` returned `simultaneous` with it; the fixed code returns `none`.
  - The frame has the same defect: with A = 1.2487618507787965, a = 2.4, g = 1.6, centre (1.5925199925867558e-115, 0), r + m = 0.07, every cell valid and the slot (-0.8509905193769635, 0.8333333333333333), the committed code accepted the slot although its extreme maps to x = 1.0000000000000002.
  - The same on y: with A = 1.2239953768539125, a = 1.0000000000000027, g = 1.6 and centre (0, 0.9999999999999981), r + m = 0.18, cell (16, 12) not valid and the slot (0.9485964170617822, 0.4615996917902612), the gap from the camera centre y = 0.6335787785319612 to the cell's edge y = 13 / 24 is 0.09191211186529458, between the distances 0.09191211186529447 (+R side) and 0.09191211186529469 (-R side). This was found after the fix, as a mutant that survived.
  - Exact tangency is not rare in these inputs. The generators draw A, a, g, r and m at the ends of their ranges, and the added slots sit at the centres of valid cells: over the 120,000 inputs, 847 slots were exactly tangent to a non-valid cell, by the test of the committed code, and 536 more were within 1e-9 of it without being exact; the committed code accepted 20 of those 536.
- Fix: `cameraEllipse` takes the four extreme points `toCamera(slot -+ (R, 0))` and `toCamera(slot -+ (0, R))`; `inFrame` compares their coordinates themselves with 0 and 1; and each semi-axis is the larger of the two distances from the centre to its extremes. Design §9.2 steps 1 and 2 and its closing paragraph say so. On seeds 1 to 120 the fixed code accepts 47,260 slots against 47,263 for the committed one, drops 3 and adds none, and the strict sampled layer found no bad point among 11,661,088.
- Regression tests, in `packages/marrow/src/core/placement.test.ts`: the seed 29 input with its exact numbers, and the y case. Run against `placement.ts` as committed in "Add slot placement", exactly these two of the 26 tests in the file fail, and the other 24 pass. The frame case above has no test of its own, as agreed: with only the frame test taken back to the committed one (centre -+ the +R-side distance), all 26 tests pass in three runs. The frame case was later pinned by the test "regression: a disc whose sample at theta = pi maps to camera x = 1.0000000000000002 leaves the frame, though the centre plus the distance to the +R side extreme stays inside" in `packages/marrow/src/core/placement.test.ts`, added in "Pin the placement frame test".
- Fix commit: "Add placement property tests".
## 2026-11-20 - Travel: a paused step after the cross-fade switch moved the cover
- Seed: 774323422, found by `travel across motion level changes` (`travelLevels.test.ts`, 50 runs, levels runs of 1000 to 10000 steps). The first failing step is index 69 of a Full journey from 0 to 1 in 5000 ms, with a level change to Reduced after the switch. It is a paused step (delta 1 ms, not added). The cover falls from 1 to 0.9999992407 at p = 0.5255 (the bound is 1e-12).
- What broke: the switch step forced `cover = 1`, while the cover formula gives less than 1 for every q above 0.5. The next step then recomputed the cover from the formula, so a paused step could move it, and a step after the switch could move it by up to about 0.21 with 100 ms deltas (`smootherstep(2 * dt / X)`). Req 8.4 (cover 1 in the switch step) and Req 8.12 (cover change at most `COVER_SLOPE_MAX * dt / X` in every step) could not both hold with the forced value.
- Fix: the cover is the formula with a plateau, `smootherstep(min(1, (1 - abs(2q - 1)) / (1 - 2h)))` with `h = maxClockStepMs / minFadeMs`, which is exactly 1 for `abs(q - 0.5) <= h`. A step crosses `q = 0.5` by at most `h`, so the switch step is on the plateau without a forced value. `COVER_SLOPE_MAX` is 5.625. Steps without time return the stored progress, distance and cover (only the level change of stage 1 applies). Req 8.4 and 8.12 and design 4.3, 4.4 and 5.1 say so.
- Regression tests: `travel across motion level changes: regression seed 774323422` in `travelLevels.test.ts`, which runs the level-run property with this seed. Against the travel.ts that had the forced cover, it fails, and it passes with the fix. Also `travel unit rules: a paused or bad-delta step returns the stored progress, distance and cover unchanged (7.6, 7.8)` and `travel unit rules: the cover is exactly 1 on the plateau |q - 0.5| <= h and falls outside it (8.4)`, and `travel unit rules: after the fade switch, a paused step keeps the cover and the next step stays within the slope bound (8.4, 8.12; docs/bugs-found.md 774323422)` in `travel.test.ts`.
- Fix commit: "Add world-view travel".

## 2026-11-20 - Ease: smootherstep rounded above 1 near 1, so the travel cover could exceed 1
- Seed: 692136 for the new property `smootherstep output stays in 0..1: property over [0, 1] with inputs near 0 and near 1` against the unclamped `ease.ts` (counterexample 0.999999). The bug was first seen by `world-view.spec.ts` step 13, reduced fade: recorded frame 19, `phase` travelling, `travel` 0, `cover` 1.0000000000000013, where the step expects exactly 1 (Req 8.4).
- What broke: `smootherstep` in `packages/world-view/src/core/ease.ts` evaluates the polynomial in Horner form. For an input just below 1, the rounding can give a value above 1: `smootherstep(0.999999986952898)` returned 1.000000000000002 (checked from a copy of the unclamped file, with the repo file's SHA-256 unchanged). `fadeCover` in `travel.ts` passes its value through without a clamp, so the cover of a fade could exceed 1 by about 2e-15. `lock.ts` uses the same function.
- Regression tests, in `packages/world-view/src/core/ease.test.ts`: `smootherstep output stays in 0..1: fixed input 0.999999986952898 (docs/bugs-found.md)` (failed on the unclamped file with 1.000000000000002) and `smootherstep output stays in 0..1: property over [0, 1] with inputs near 0 and near 1` (failed on the unclamped file at seed 692136). The per-step bound `0 <= cover <= 1` was added to `travelLevels.test.ts`. Both level-run tests, including the regression seed 774323422, passed on the unclamped file, so the level-run property did not catch this bug; the fixed-input block and the property did.
- Fix: the polynomial's result is clamped to [0, 1] in `smootherstep`. The Horner form and the input clamp are unchanged. The clamp in `travel.ts` stage 4 is left as it was.
- Fix commit: "Clamp smootherstep output to 0..1".
## 2026-11-24 - Travel test: the 7.11 generator drew a segment of about 3e-19 ms
- Seed: 871. The 7.11 property `travel across motion level changes: constant Full and Gentle are monotone, in range and ease in and out` (`travel.test.ts`) failed once in a full run at garden-level task 6, commit D, and that run's seed was not logged. Seed 871 is the first seed of 1 to 871 that reproduces it with the unbounded generator (scan with `fc.assert` at 50 runs each). The shrunk counterexample is `{ from: 0, to: 1, durationMs: 3.1763735522036268e-19 }`, `full`, share 0.5.
- What broke: the test generator, not `travel.ts`. `genSegment` in `testgen.ts` draws a door duration as `doorMs * share`, and `share` can be close to 0. A segment that short leaves no step in the middle band of the ease check, so `Math.max` over that empty list is `-Infinity`. The shortest durations the design uses are a door (`doorMs`, 3000 ms) and a journey (`journeyMinMs`, 5000 ms).
- Fix: the 7.11 property draws `genSegment()` filtered to `durationMs >= TUNING.doorMs`. Its assertions are unchanged. `genSegment` itself is unchanged, so the other travel properties still draw short door fractions.
- Regression test: `travel across motion level changes: regression seed 871, a segment of 3e-19 ms (7.11, docs/bugs-found.md)` in `travel.test.ts`, the same property with `seed: 871`. With the unbounded generator, seed 871 fails. With the bound, seed 871 passes, and seeds 1 to 2000 (50 runs each) gave no failure.
- Fix commit: "Bound the segment duration in the travel step property".
